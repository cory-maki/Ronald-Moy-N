About Ronald

A Career Built on Patient Capital

Ronald Moy spent his career as a real estate entrepreneur in Los Angeles, one of the most competitive property markets in the United States. His approach rested on strategic acquisitions and patient capital rather than speculation, a commitment to a single asset class sustained over several decades.

That commitment carried him through more than one full market cycle. Expansions and contractions both left their mark on Southern California real estate, and Moy's record reflects an investor who learned to separate short-term disruption from long-term value. The story of his career in Los Angeles real estate traces how that discipline took shape.

Now retired, Ronald focuses on mentorship and knowledge transfer, sharing the perspective of a full career with professionals who are earlier in theirs. His essay on the long arc of Los Angeles entrepreneurship is a natural starting point.
